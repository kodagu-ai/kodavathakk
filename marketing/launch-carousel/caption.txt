Our language has no script. It lives in the voice — and today we begin saving it. 🪔

UNESCO calls Kodava Takk definitely endangered. Every year, fewer children answer their grandmothers in Thakk. So today we launch KODAVA THAKK — a community-owned movement to preserve our language as sound, and teach AI to help pass it on.

→ Record a few minutes of Thakk in your browser
→ A live dial counts every voice and hour
→ Credited to your name, okka, and village
→ Held under a Kodava guardianship licence — never sold

🎙️ Give your voice: kodavathakk.kodagu.ai

Then send this to the family WhatsApp — and ask your eldest relative to speak first. That recording is an heirloom.
